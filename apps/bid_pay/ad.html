For customers

Pay less for food and delivery.

  • Set the delivery price yourself. Raise it if you're in a hurry.
  • Pay the restaurant's real price, with no markup added by us.
  • Members pay a $0 service fee on every order.
  • See any driver counter-offer and accept it only if you like it.
For drivers

Earn more. Share less.

  • See the customer's price before you accept. No surprises.
  • Pick the jobs worth your time, or send a counter-offer.
  • Members keep 100% of every bid. A flat membership replaces the cut.
