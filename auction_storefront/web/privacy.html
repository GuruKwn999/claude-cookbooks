Privacy policy

Last updated September 16, 2026

This is a one-person shop, and the data it holds on you is exactly what it needs to sell you a single physical object and get it to you — nothing is collected to build a profile or to sell to anyone else.

1. What's collected, and why

DataCollected whenUsed for
Name, email, shipping addressCheckoutFulfilling and communicating about your order

2. Payment data specifically

Card numbers, bank details and wallet credentials are entered directly into our payment processor's own hosted fields (Stripe). This site's servers never receive, store or transmit a card number — the checkout code is written so that it structurally cannot. What we do receive from the processor is limited to the outcome (paid or not) and enough metadata to match it to your order.

3. The curator

The on-page assistant answers from the public catalogue. When a server is configured, your question and the recent conversation are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a reply; Anthropic's own terms govern how that request is handled. Without a server configured, the curator answers entirely in your browser and nothing about your question leaves your device.

4. Who else sees it

  • Stripe — payment processing, fraud prevention, and (if enabled) tax calculation.
  • Anthropic — curator questions, only when the curator is server-backed.
  • Shipping carriers — name and address, for lots that ship or go by freight.
  • Nobody else. No data broker, no ad network, no sale of personal information.

5. How long it's kept

Order records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires in the seller's jurisdiction, typically several years. Cart contents live only in your browser's local storage and are never uploaded. Curator conversations held server-side are kept only long enough to answer your question and are not used to train a model.

6. Your choices

Email the address in the footer to ask what we hold on you, correct it, or have it deleted, subject to what tax law requires us to retain. Clearing your browser's site data removes your cart and any remembered language, currency and theme preference immediately — those never touch a server.
