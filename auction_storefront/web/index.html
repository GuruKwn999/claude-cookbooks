How it works

Three steps, and the second one is the whole business.

Anyone can buy at auction. The part that decides whether you get a fair deal is what gets written down between the sale room and the shelf.

Bought at the block

I bid in person at regional sale rooms, estate clearances and fleet disposals. The lot number, the sale and the house stay attached to the piece for life — you can see all three on every listing.

Graded against a fixed scale

A to D, applied the same way whether it is a teapot or a Mercedes. Grade D means "as found" and is priced as a project. Faults go in the lot note in plain language: the dent, the hairline, the cambelt history.

Fulfilled for what it is

Small pieces ship. Furniture goes by quoted freight. Cars and motorcycles are collection only, against a deposit. Property is sold by private treaty — never through a cart.

Shipping & fulfilment

Four ways a lot reaches you, because one box doesn't fit a house.

Small pieces ship by courier. Furniture and tiny homes go by quoted freight. Vehicles are collection only, against a deposit. Property is sold by private treaty and never touches a cart at all.

Shipped

Tracked and insured courier, charged at checkout. Rates below are for lots under 20 kg.

Freight

Furniture, pallets and tiny homes. Quoted after checkout — nothing moves until you approve the cost.

Collection

Cars, motorcycles and commercial vehicles. A deposit reserves it; the balance is due when you collect.

Private treaty

Property and land. Register your interest and the owner replies directly — no cart, no deposit online.
