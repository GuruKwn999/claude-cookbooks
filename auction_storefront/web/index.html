How it works

Three steps, and the second one is the whole business.

Anyone can buy at auction. The part that decides whether you get a fair deal is what gets written down between the sale room and the shelf.

Bought at the block

I bid in person at regional sale rooms and estate clearances. The lot number, the sale and the house stay attached to the piece for life — you can see all three on every listing.

Graded against a fixed scale

A to D, applied the same way every time. Grade D means "as found" and is priced as a project, not as a bargain. Faults go in the lot note in plain language: the dent, the hairline, the two missing marcasites.

Packed for the object, not the box

Glass double-boxed, textiles rolled not folded, furniture palletised and quoted separately. Tracked and insured for the full purchase price, every time, with the real value on the customs form.
