Terms of sale

Last updated September 16, 2026

These terms govern a purchase made through Pamela Y. Logan Industries. Reading them matters more here than in most shops: every lot is a single object bought at a real auction, and how a sale is formed differs by what you're buying.

1. What you're buying

Every lot is one physical object. There is no restock, no "similar item," and no guarantee a comparable piece will appear again. The lot number, the sale room and the auction it came from are stated on every listing and are part of what you're paying for.

2. How a sale is formed, by fulfilment type

  • Shipped lots — the sale is formed when payment is captured at checkout, for the full price shown.
  • Freight lots (furniture, tiny homes, pallets) — the sale is formed at checkout for the item price; carriage is quoted separately afterward and nothing ships until you approve that quote.
  • Collection lots (vehicles) — the amount charged at checkout is a deposit, not the price. It reserves the lot and is non-refundable once you have inspected it and confirmed the purchase, per Section 4. The balance is due, in full, before or at collection. If you do not complete within 14 days of the deposit without an agreed extension, the deposit is forfeited and the lot is relisted.
  • Enquiry lots (property and land) — nothing is sold through this website. Registering interest opens a private conversation with the owner; any resulting sale is a separate, offline transaction with its own contract, conducted through the appropriate legal process for real property in the relevant jurisdiction.

3. Price, currency and tax

Prices are set in US dollars and converted for display in the currency you select; the conversion shown while browsing is indicative. The amount actually charged is computed at checkout, in the settlement currency, from the US dollar price. Sales tax, VAT or GST is added where required by law for your jurisdiction. Import duty and destination-country taxes on shipped or freighted lots are the buyer's responsibility and are not included in the price or the shipping quote.

4. Vehicle inspection

Every vehicle listing states its odometer reading, title status and whether it runs and drives, honestly and to the best of the seller's knowledge — but a vehicle is sold as inspected, not as warranted. You are strongly encouraged to inspect a vehicle, or have it inspected, before the deposit becomes non-refundable. Once you confirm the purchase after inspection, the sale is final subject to Section 6.

5. Payment

We accept the major card networks, digital wallets, and buy-now-pay-later providers shown at checkout for your country; availability varies by destination and is determined by our payment processor at the time of checkout. A card, wallet or BNPL charge that is declined or reversed by your provider voids the sale of that lot.

6. Cancellation and returns

Shipped and freighted lots graded A through C may be returned within 14 days of delivery; grade D lots are sold "as found" and are final. Vehicle deposits are non-refundable once the buyer has confirmed the purchase after inspection. Full detail is in our returns and condition policy.

7. Limitation

Condition grades and lot notes reflect the seller's honest assessment at the time of listing, not a professional appraisal or a warranty of authenticity, fitness for a particular purpose, or future value. Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot be excluded by law.

8. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the seller's home jurisdiction, without regard to conflict-of-law principles. This is placeholder text — have counsel confirm the correct governing-law clause for where you actually trade before you rely on it.
