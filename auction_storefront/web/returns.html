Returns & condition policy

Last updated September 16, 2026

The condition grade on a listing is a promise, not a suggestion. This page says what each grade means and what happens if the piece doesn't match it — and, separately, what your options are if you simply change your mind.

1. The grading scale

GradeMeaningReturnable?
AMint — no wear found on inspectionYes, 14 days
A-Near mint — trivial, disclosed wearYes, 14 days
BExcellent — light, honest useYes, 14 days
B-Very good — visible but soundYes, 14 days
CGood, honest wear — used as intended, for yearsYes, 14 days
DAs found — sold as a project, faults not fully resolvedNo — final sale

Grade D exists so a piece that needs work can still be sold honestly, at a price that reflects it, without either side pretending otherwise. That is also why it carries no right of return on condition grounds — you are buying exactly what the listing describes, faults included.

2. Returns, grades A–C

Shipped and freighted lots graded A through C may be returned within 14 days of delivery, for any reason. Return shipping is the buyer's unless the item arrived not matching its listed condition or grade, in which case return shipping is ours and we arrange collection. Refunds are issued to the original payment method once the item is received back in the condition it was sent.

3. If a lot arrives misdescribed

If a shipped or freighted lot arrives with damage or a condition issue not disclosed in the listing, email us within 48 hours with photographs. We will arrange a full refund including original shipping, and cover return shipping, regardless of grade — including grade D, if what's wrong is something beyond what the listing described.

4. Vehicle deposits

The amount charged at checkout on a vehicle lot is a deposit that reserves it for inspection, not the purchase price. If, on inspection, the vehicle does not match its listing, the deposit is refunded in full. If it does match and you decide not to proceed anyway, the deposit is non-refundable — it compensates for taking the vehicle off the market while you inspected it. This is stated at checkout before the deposit is charged.

5. Property enquiries

Registering interest in a property or land lot commits you to nothing and charges nothing. No deposit is taken through this website for property; any deposit or contract is arranged directly with the owner through the normal legal process for real property in the relevant jurisdiction, and this policy does not apply to it.

6. How to start a return

Email the address in the footer with your order number and lot number. We reply within two business days with a return label where one applies.
